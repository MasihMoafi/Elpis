ACE · controlled context

Compress the noise.
Keep the evidence.

When experimental Smart Prune is explicitly enabled, ACE can rewrite completed tool output before its first main-model admission. The tool-call envelope stays intact and already-sent history does not change.

Continuity contract

The runtime infers.
Elpis carries the work.

A lean checkpoint is not a provider’s native conversation history. Elpis keeps the durable working agreement inspectable while exact resume remains provider-native.

Research in progress

Still being
evaluated.

Smart Prune is experimental. Early synthetic studies explore its costs and tradeoffs; they do not establish reliable savings or improved coding quality in real projects.

The research notes include measured results, failed probes, limitations, and the distinction between reported tests and independently repeated checks.

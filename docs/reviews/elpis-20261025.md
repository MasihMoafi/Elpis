**RQ3 task performance — exploratory results and future work**

Historical records identify **nine named real tasks**: seven Terminal-Bench tasks/eight paired comparisons (Terra medium, Luna Low pruner), plus two older LHTB tasks with model settings unavailable. No clear, consistent performance difference was established. [Recorded tasks](evidence-20261025/rq3-historical-tasks.json).

Resource limits prevented systematic tests of demanding tasks with Fable, Opus, or Astra and an inexpensive or free pruner. That remains [future work](../evals/rq3/FINAL_PROTOCOL.md).

The [initial Sol/Luna pilot](../evals/rq3/SOL_LUNA_PILOT_20261025.md) recorded six runs on three tasks, including two verifier timeouts. A [four-run recovery](../evals/rq3/RECOVERY_20261025.md) completed the affected pairs: web-server 0/0 OFF/ON; SQLite 1/1, with seven verified admissions. Original errors remain recorded; one grade resumed without another model call. **No difference in task success was observed on these two tasks.** This is a small exploratory comparison.

Recorded input-plus-output usage, including cached input, was **703,692 tokens OFF versus 1,250,509 ON**, including 37,321 Luna tokens: **77.7% more with pruning**. Different rollouts and shared cache state limit interpretation. Dollar cost was not measured. No token or monetary savings were demonstrated in this recovery cohort.

# SPEC: Elpis ready for production

This spec lists what "ready for production" means for Elpis, item by item, each with its check and its state. Goal:
Elpis can be shipped and trusted as a daily driver. Every check is green; the router and Workflows do what they
promise in the installed app; checks, not memory, enforce the shared design and behaviour system; and the repository
reads in order. Masih, 10 Nov: "Finalize this project for production, make that shared-system stick, make sure the
workflows + router work as intended." The finish line, in his words: "when I won't need to work on it anymore and
could use it with the flow": he talks to the router, and it runs and reports his sessions.

Invariants: no chat, ticket, board or setting of his is lost; `main`, tags, releases and deploys change only with his
yes; no traffic goes through his VPN without his yes (live tests start the app with every proxy off).
Constraints: Rust builds on GitHub Actions; one heavy job at a time.

Status words: `todo`, `doing`, `done: <evidence>`, `his call` (waits for Masih). "Installed app" below means
`~/.local/bin/elpis-app`, driven through its window on 10 and 11 Nov with every proxy off.
